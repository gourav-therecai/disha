Maps and data

With your permission, Disha obtains your browser location to centre the map and find nearby businesses. Google receives the area and radius needed for map search.

Disha retains Google Place IDs for linked leads. Business details you enter, plans, visits, optional visit photos, and learning progress are stored in your account. Google search results remain in the current session.

See Google Maps Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Disha sends the saved business category, suggested role and selected catalog product to OpenAI to draft an English and Hindi visit pitch. The generated scripts are saved with the lead.
